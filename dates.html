Important dates

Our 2026–27 conference season, from first feedback at Regionals to the national stage.

Every member's season follows the same path: Regionals, then States, with Nationals open to those who qualify. Here's what each stage means and what's expected of you.

Countdown to Regionals
–Days
–Hours
–Mins
–Secs
1 Required
Regionals — Feb 12
East Carolina University

All members are required to attend Regionals. Event submissions, where applicable, must be completed by this date. The main purpose of Regionals is to get valuable feedback so you can improve your project before States.

2 Required
States — Apr 15–17
Koury Convention Center

All members are required to attend States. Placing in the top 3 of your event at States qualifies you to advance to Nationals.

3 Optional · encouraged
Nationals — Jun 23–27
Rosen Shingle Creek, Orlando

Nationals is optional but highly encouraged. Only members who place in the top 3 of their event at States are eligible to attend.
