ABOUT THE CLUB

What is TSA?

TSA is a national Career and Technical Student Organization of middle and high school students who engage in events in science, public speaking, engineering, technology, mathematics, and more to find their passions for their futures.

Members don't just study STEM — they apply it. Through ideating, community service, teamwork, and problem solving, chapters like ours turn classroom concepts into real projects, portfolios, and competition entries. Every event is a chance to try on a different kind of technical work and see what fits.

TSA offers 42 high school events for the 2026–2027 year — spanning engineering, design, coding, media, and leadership, so there's a real path in for almost any interest.

WHY WE EXIST

Mission & vision

Mission Statement

The Technology Student Association fosters personal growth, leadership, and opportunities in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM); members apply and integrate these concepts through co-curricular activities, competitions, and related programs.

Vision Statement

The Technology Student Association is accelerating student achievement and supporting teachers by providing engaging opportunities to develop STEM skills.

FOR MEMBERS

What you get out of it

A CTSO membership is more than a line on a college application — it's a low-stakes place to practice the skills that make the rest of high school (and beyond) easier.

Competition experience

Regional and state conferences give you real feedback on real projects — from engineering builds to debate rounds — judged by people working in those fields.

Leadership & community

Officer roles, team-based events, and required community service give members a chance to lead, collaborate, and give back well before graduation.

SINCE 1958

TSA history

The Technology Student Association, formerly the American Industrial Arts Student Association (AIASA), is the oldest student membership organization dedicated exclusively to students enrolled in technology and engineering education classes in middle and high schools.

1958–78

A sponsored activity

AIASA operated as a sponsored activity of the American Industrial Arts Association (AIAA).

1978

AIASA, Inc. is formed

The nonprofit corporation AIASA, Inc. was formed to oversee the organization on its own, separate from AIAA.

1978–88

A decade of growth

The organization grew in size, strength, structure, and impact on students and secondary school programs.

1988

Becomes TSA

In the summer of 1988, AIASA officially became the Technology Student Association.

Source: tsaweb.org/about/history

National service partner

TSA currently holds a national service partnership with the American Red Cross, giving chapters a shared community service focus alongside their competitive events.
