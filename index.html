What is TSA?

TSA is a national Career and Technical Student Organization of middle and high school students who compete in events across science, public speaking, engineering, technology, mathematics, and more — helping members find their passions for the future.

Every year, chapters like ours take part in a full slate of competitive events that cover almost every corner of STEM. For the 2026–2027 year, TSA is offering 42 high school events, ranging from coding and engineering design to debate, video production, and biotechnology — there is genuinely something for every interest.

Through ideating, community service, teamwork, and problem solving, our motto is simple:

"Learning to Lead in a Technical World."

Why join Green Level TSA?

  • Compete in real STEM events, from engineering and coding to design and public speaking
  • Build leadership experience through officer roles, committees, and chapter projects
  • Strengthen a college application or résumé with regional, state, and national recognition
  • Give back through community service tied to our national service partner
  • Work alongside a team that shares your interest in technology and problem solving

Mission & vision

Mission statement

The Technology Student Association fosters personal growth, leadership, and opportunities in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM). Members apply and integrate these concepts through co-curricular activities, competitions, and related programs.

Vision statement

The Technology Student Association is accelerating student achievement and supporting teachers by providing engaging opportunities to develop STEM skills.

TSA history

The Technology Student Association, formerly the American Industrial Arts Student Association (AIASA), is the oldest student membership organization dedicated exclusively to students enrolled in technology and engineering education classes in middle and high schools. Its history spans more than four decades.

1958–1978

AIASA operates as a sponsored activity of the American Industrial Arts Association (AIAA).

1978

AIASA, Inc. is formed as a nonprofit corporation to oversee AIASA as its own independent organization.

1978–1988

The organization grows in size, strength, and structure, expanding its impact on students and secondary school programs nationwide.

1988

AIASA is renamed the Technology Student Association — the name and mission our chapter carries on today.

Source: tsaweb.org/about/history

National service partner

TSA currently holds a national service partnership with the American Red Cross, connecting chapters like ours to community service opportunities nationwide.
